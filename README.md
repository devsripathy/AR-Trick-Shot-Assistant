## 🧠 The Idea

You know that moment in *Infinite Mage* when Shirone does **insane mental math** in his head—calculating trajectories, velocities, angles, air resistance, spin—all in real-time, and then casually pulls off something impossible?

**Yeah. This is that. But for trick shots.**

**AR Trickshot AI** takes that **one core concept**—**doing complicated calculations in your head to manipulate reality**—and turns it into an augmented reality assistant.

No deep lore. No Spirit Zones. No mana pools.  
Just **"what if I could calculate the perfect shot in real-time and project it onto my AR glasses?"**

It's **math as magic**.  
It's **weeb engineering at its finest**.  
It's **glorified physics cheating**.  
And it's ridiculously fun.
